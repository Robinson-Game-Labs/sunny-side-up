# Sunny Side Up

A daily good news app: 5 Mississippi Coast stories (within ~100 miles of Biloxi), 5 national and 5 global, with a "start here" top 3, summaries and links to the original articles.

`stories.json` is refreshed every morning around 6 AM Central. The page itself is `index.html`.

To install on a phone, open the site and choose **Add to Home Screen**.
